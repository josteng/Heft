Heft comes to iPhone and iPad, with the same live editor, search and calendar as on the Mac, and the Mac gets a properties table, an Outline, Share, muted colors and a long list of fixes for notes written elsewhere.

## iPhone and iPad

Install it from [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/sDBqe2Ws). Pick your vault folder in Files once; iCloud Drive keeps it in sync with the Mac, as before. It is the same vault Obsidian opens, so each device can use either app.

- **Notes open in the Mac's live editor**: markup hides as you read and shows at the caret, tasks tick with a tap, and links, tags and pictures draw as on the Mac.
- **A keyboard strip as in Notes**: Aa for headings and styles, tasks, lists, links, tags, quotes, highlights in color, and photos or files from the library, the camera or Files.
- **Typing completes as on the Mac**: `[[` links with their headings and aliases, `#` tags, `==` highlight colors and `> [!` callouts. Replacements such as `->` and closing pairs come from the Mac's Typing settings, through the vault.
- **Search is the Mac's ⌘T**: notes, commands, tags, folders and text in one field, with the same `@`, `>`, `#` and `/` scopes and pins.
- **Recent, Files and Calendar**: the day's note sits under a month that folds to its week as you read.
- **The iPad has the Mac's layout**: a sidebar, the note beside it, an Outline and Linked From panel, Back and Forward, and the menu bar with the Mac's shortcuts. Apple Pencil writes with Scribble, and a squeeze opens the format palette.
- **Capture from anywhere**: a quick sheet for the inbox or today's note, from Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, the Home Screen icon and the share sheet. A Today widget shows today's note.
- **The properties table, rename with suggested names, move, delete with links repointed, and agent proposals to review**, as on the Mac.
- **Handoff** passes the open note between the Mac and iOS, and Spotlight and Shortcuts open notes.
- **Pull a list down to run a command**, by default Capture or New Note, chosen in Settings ▸ Browsing. Capture suggests the tag a line reads like, from the lines you have tagged before.
- **Changes from the Mac arrive while Heft is open**, and are merged into what you are typing; nothing typed is lost to a conflict or a closed app. Markdown files in your vault open from the Files app.
- **Settings** keep the vault's colors, pins and typing rules shared with the Mac; what stays on one device says so.

## Added

- **Frontmatter is a properties table**, as in Obsidian: each property on a row with a type icon, list items as pills, keys and values completed from the vault, and problems in red with the reason. Right-click a row for its type, Cut, Copy, Paste and Remove. View ▸ Properties as Text shows the YAML; Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Properties can show it always or start folded.
- **Outline in the right sidebar (⌥⌘3)** lists the note's headings and lights the one you are in; click one to go there.
- **Share from a file's menu in the sidebar**, and **Heft in the Share menu** of Safari and other apps: to today's note, the inbox or a note you pick, with the page's title offered as the link's text.
- **Click a tag** to open it in the sidebar's Tags, or in ⌘T when Tags is hidden. A tag's menu in the sidebar has Search in #tag and Copy Tag.
- **Colors can be Vivid or Muted**, every color is a row of swatches, and Appearance previews the editor as it will look.
- **Headings get settings of their own**: New York as their font, and the level-color bar on or off.
- **Click the calendar's month to fold it to this week**; the arrows then page by week.
- **Accepting a proposal opens its note**; turn it off in Settings ▸ General.
- **Your colors, pins and typing rules are kept in the vault's `.heft` folder**, so every Mac and iPhone on that vault shares them.
- **`heft capture --done`** writes a ticked task, and **`--at`** files the line at another time, in time order in the daily note and under its own day in the inbox.

## Changed

- **Heft has a new identifier, `dev.josteng.Heft`**, shared by the Mac and the iPhone. Your settings come along. Open Heft once after updating, then add again any Shortcuts and Spotlight quick keys made with its actions.
- **Waiting for Review is docked at the sidebar's foot**, with the calendar stacked under it, and a decided change slides out of the review.
- **The proposal banner and the find bar float over the note** instead of pushing it down.
- **Tasks are drawn as circles**, filled with a tick when done.
- **`#` offers tags only once a letter follows it**, so typing a heading no longer opens the menu.
- **Shift-Return breaks the line without starting a list item**, as in Obsidian.
- **The sidebar's menus are shorter**: Ask About, Focus Window and Open in New Window no longer repeat the item's name.

## Fixed

- **Notes written on Windows** (CRLF) keep their line endings when you type, paste, accept a proposal or use Vim's `yy p`, and their frontmatter, tables, code fences, headings and block links are read again.
- **A note with a byte-order mark or in Latin-1** keeps its bytes on save, and is indexed and searchable.
- **A note changed while it is open**, by a capture from Spotlight or another device, no longer jumps back to the top.
- **A capture or a change from another device while you type** is merged into the note instead of raising a conflict, and ⌘Z no longer undoes into the wrong line afterward.
- **Capturing into a note iCloud has offloaded** no longer replaces it with a new one, and the inbox's frontmatter stays at its top.
- **Symlinked notes and folders** are indexed and kept up to date.
- **Renaming a note** by a change of case alone works, a `/` typed into the name stays in the name, and `[[Note]]` links keep pointing at it after a move.
- **`->` and the other replacements** work again after a price such as `5$`.
- **Pictures stay clickable** after typing elsewhere in the note.
- **Ctrl-Return** types a plain line break instead of a character no Markdown reader takes.
- **The pointer stays a hand over a task's box.**
- **Accepting a proposal** keeps a missing final newline, keeps a half-reviewed group together, refuses a change that no longer fits the note, and never writes outside the vault.
- **Ask** keeps a deleted chat deleted, saves a chat in the vault it was asked in, and no longer stalls one chat while another is quiet. Its `heft` calls stay inside the vault and folder they were given.
- **Several `heft` verbs refuse input they used to misread**, such as `rename --now` taken as the name or `daily 26-03-02` as the year 26.
