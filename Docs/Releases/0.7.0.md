One unified search bar under ⌘T finds notes, commands, tags and text, an experimental Ask answers questions about your notes, Apple Intelligence suggests names, and the editor learns colored highlights and more.

## Added

- **⌘T opens one search bar for notes, commands, tags, folders and the text inside notes**, with what you opened last and use most listed first. `@`, `>`, `#` and `/` narrow it to one kind, Backspace widens it again, and ⌘1 to ⌘5 switch between all five in that order.
- **Pin a note, folder, tag or command with ⌘D** in the bar, or from its right-click menu. Pinned items come first and sync with the vault, and a pinned note follows a rename.
- **Right-click a result in the bar** to rename, move, trash or copy the path of a note, or to focus a window on a folder, as in the file tree.
- **Settings ▸ Search sets what ⌘T lists before you type**: recent, frequent or pinned items in any order, and whether a folder's or a tag's notes sort by use, by name or by last edit.
- **Apple Intelligence suggests names, on device**: a note's name when you rename it, a link's text after `|`, short titles for a pasted web address, and a name for each Ask chat. Each can be turned off in Settings.
- **Ask (experimental, off by default)** runs your own Claude Code from the search bar (⌘6 or `?`) or the right sidebar, and the edits it wants arrive as proposals you accept or reject in the chat. It needs Claude Code installed and signed in: Heft adds no account or subscription, and use counts against your Claude plan. Turn it on in Settings ▸ Ask.
- **Ask About** on a note, a folder or selected text starts a chat about just that. Chats are kept and listed in ⌘T.
- **Rename a note from the window's title** with a click or ⇧⌘R, or by force-clicking its row in the sidebar.
- **⌘1 to ⌘3 switch the sidebar's views**, which can be hidden and reordered in Settings ▸ General.
- **Typing `==` offers Obsidian's six highlight colors**, drawn in the editor, the reading view and PDF export. Click a highlight's dot, or right-click it, to change its color.
- **Click a picture to select it**, then drag its corner or press + and - to resize it, 0 to reset it, and Space or a double-click to open it in Quick Look.
- **`![300](x.png)` sets a Markdown picture's width**, as in Obsidian.
- **`[[` finds notes by the aliases in their frontmatter.** After the note's name, `|` offers its aliases and `#` offers its headings.
- **`#` completes tags** from the vault, most used first.
- **Pasting a web address on its own offers the page's title** to link it with; Settings ▸ Typing ▸ Suggest Link Titles turns this off, since it loads the page. Pasting one over selected text makes the selection a link.
- **The command palette has Insert callout and the list commands**: toggle a bullet or numbered list, indent and unindent.
- **⌘L on an empty line starts a task**, as in Obsidian.
- **`heft search` ranks notes, folders and tags as ⌘T does**, `heft files --sort used|recent|edited|name` lists them in the bar's orders, and `heft chats` reads past Ask chats.

## Changed

- **Quick open (⌘O), the command palette (⌘P) and vault search (⇧⌘F)** now open the search bar, already narrowed to notes, commands or text.
- **Backlinks share the right side with Ask**: ⌥⌘0 shows or hides it instead of ⌥⌘B, and ⌥⌘1 and ⌥⌘2 switch between the two.
- **Settings ▸ Startup sets what a window reopened from the Dock shows**, by default the note it was last on.
- **Reveal in Sidebar is now Reveal in Files** (⇧⌘J).
- **Tab and Shift-Tab indent and unindent every list item in a selection.**
- **A proposed move reads by names**: "Move Plan to Archive" rather than "Move Projects/Plan.md to Archive/Plan.md".
- **The completion menu animates as you type**, and stays still under Reduce Motion.
- **The file tree scrolls smoothly** through a folder of thousands of notes.

## Fixed

- **⌘B over partly bold text** no longer nests the markers; it makes one bold span, as Obsidian does.
- **⌘Z right after `->` turns into `→`** gives back `->` instead of leaving `-`.
- **Substitutions such as `->`** work again right after a web address or a tag.
- **Clicking beside a checkbox or bullet** puts the caret in the text instead of in front of the hidden marker.
- **A link whose text has escaped brackets**, such as `[\[Shop\] Sale](url)`, is now drawn as a link.
- **Capture actions in Spotlight and Shortcuts** no longer go missing after Heft is updated in place.
- **⌘L, Rename Note and Reveal in Files** no longer stay grayed out after launch while a note is open.
- **Opening Settings** no longer puts the caret in the pane's first text field, and a new window no longer puts it in the sidebar's filter.
