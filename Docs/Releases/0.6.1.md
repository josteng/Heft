Pasted tasks keep their tick, Cancel in the save conflict alert means cancel, and the Recent list and formatting bar behave better.

## Changed

- When a note was removed outside Heft while you were editing it, the alert offers **Discard My Changes**, which closes the note.

## Fixed

- A `- - -` rule stays a rule when the caret moves onto its line, instead of turning into a bullet.
- Pasting a task onto a task line leaves one checkbox, the pasted one, so a ticked task stays ticked.
- **Cancel** in the save conflict alert closes it for good; saving is still paused, and ⌘S (Save) asks again.
- Filtering the Recent list starts it at the top instead of where it was scrolled.
- The pointer is an arrow over the formatting bar, including between its buttons.
- The formatting bar no longer shows under the right-click menu.
