Ready for macOS 27: the fixes it needed, and some Liquid Glass so Heft feels at home.

## Changed

- The formatting bar that appears when selecting text is drawn in Liquid Glass, matching macOS 26 and 27.
- The formatting bar also appears in Vim Visual and Visual Line mode. **Settings ▸ Vim ▸ Show the formatting bar in Visual mode** turns it off.

## Fixed

On macOS 27:

- The **All Notes** menu above the sidebar shows its name again instead of just an arrow.
- Menus in Settings are no longer tinted with the accent colour.

And everywhere:

- The formatting bar stays in view when a selection starts above the top of the window.
- Bold, italic, strikethrough and highlight across list items, tasks, quotes and headings wrap the words, not the markers.
- After formatting in Vim Visual mode, from the bar or with ⌘B, the next command acts on the formatted words.
